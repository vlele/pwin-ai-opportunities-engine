## Architectural Change

Use one source-linked semantic plan, then audit that immutable plan. Do not ask a
second model to rewrite every claim and independently summarize fit. Distinguish
performed tasks, unresolved reference labels, general capabilities, identity,
preferences and qualifications. Compare tasks with explicit requirement atoms.
Code computes citation unions, labels, questions and rendering from those records.
Code never determines task similarity from customer names or industry keywords.

The current candidate stages inventory, code-enumerated comparison batches and
homogeneous evidence-audit batches. Partial same-task overlap remains distinct from
different-task transfer. Exact package quotes are code-owned. Supported questions
may be shown even when other judgments are rejected, but research stays blocked.

Questions reference unresolved records; they are not claims that their answers
are true. Identity resolution alone cannot establish performed tasks. An absent
history is not a hidden ambiguous project, and additional coverage is not a reason
to downgrade an identifiable direct partial match to transferable experience.

Claims now declare `unresolved_dimensions` separately from broad attribution and
claim-form labels. No implicit legal-identity question follows from unclear duties.
A single source-bound question channel owns warrant decisions across initial
detection and inventory. Exact duplicate candidates share an immutable receipt;
modern assertion auditors cannot vote again on question warrant. Rejecting an
extra question does not erase valid classifications. Rejecting every question
while retaining an unresolved claim is a contract conflict, not permission to
research. Pending questions survive unrelated provider/audit failures as pending.

## Frozen Acceptance

- Unit and integration negatives reject invented quotations, wrong source roles,
  missing comparisons, duplicate IDs, unsupported positive credit, and dropped
  unresolved references. Provider and semantic rejection never authorize research.
- Known cases: three repeats, one explicit model snapshot and reasoning setting.
  Use low effort for cost-sensitive development unless separately approved.
  No retries for
  semantic rejection and no selecting the best repeat.
- Held-out cases: three repeats after the known gate. A failing holdout becomes
  regression data; do not tune to it and reuse it as unseen evidence.
- At least 95 percent usable checkpoints; zero critical semantic failures, zero
  fabricated experience/eligibility, and no unnecessary blocking questions on
  clear controls. Correct decisions, not predetermined pursuit scores.
- Audit adversarial plans separately: unsupported assertions must be rejected;
  honest unanswered questions and qualified partial matches must be accepted.
- Exercise resume, research boundary, missing information, source injection and
  long-package coverage offline. Live short controls do not prove PDF extraction
  or final-memo usefulness.
- Review the whole local diff, run all offline suites and check for secrets and
  source-specific rules before reporting main readiness. Do not push on failure.

## Scorecard Migration

Version 3 grades supplied work/reference records separately from absent coverage
gaps. It requires every supplied work record to satisfy the expected relationship
and no unsupported positive edge. It does not demand that a separate unknown
gap have the same label as known work. Preserve version 2 grades for comparison.
Work-reference records remain unknown until meaning and attribution are resolved.

## Stop Rule

Freeze each candidate before paid calls. Preserve raw responses, failures, usage,
hashes and manual findings. Stop the release decision on any critical regression;
never remove a negative control or soften an invariant to claim readiness. If the
remaining failure is model capability, report that rather than proliferating
solicitation-specific rules or silently increasing model cost.
